The Daily Three

A small one, a medium one, and a big one.

Every day brings three new puzzles: 10x10 for a coffee break, 15x15 for a proper sit down, and 20x20 for the evening. They start gentle on Monday and get tougher as the week goes on, so there's always one that fits the time you have.

Easy to Learn

Never played one before? You will get it in a minute.

Each number counts a run of filled squares in its row or column. That's the whole game. A five-by-five practice puzzle walks you through it, wrong squares just bounce back out, and the first picture you draw is a heart.

Day and Night

Follows your system theme.

Warm paper by day, deep ink by night. The board picks up whichever one your device is already using, so a late night puzzle never blinds you.

Everything You Need

Built for people who just want a good puzzle.

Never a Guess

Every puzzle is checked by a solver before it ships. If it cannot be finished by logic alone, it does not make it in.

Click or Drag

Fill a run in one stroke, mark the blanks with crosses, and undo or redo anything. It works the same with a mouse or a thumb.

A Nudge When Stuck

Hints fill in one square you could have worked out yourself, so you get unstuck without being handed the answer.

Streaks and Stats

A free account keeps your solves, your times, and your daily streak, with a one day grace period for the day life gets in the way.

Add to Home Screen

Install it from your browser and it opens like an app, with its own icon and no address bar in the way.

Nothing Creepy

No ads, no autocapture, no session recording. Sign in with an emailed link or a passkey: there are no passwords here at all.
