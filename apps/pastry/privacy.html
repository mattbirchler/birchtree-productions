Pastry

Privacy Policy

TLDR

Pastry collects nothing. What you copy stays on your devices. If you turn on iCloud sync, it also goes to your own iCloud account, end-to-end encrypted, where only your devices can read it.

What We Collect

Nothing. Pastry has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no servers of its own. We never see what you copy, and there is nothing of yours for us to read, sell or share.

What Stays on Your Devices

  • Your clipboard history: text, links and images
  • Your collections and the names you give items
  • The text Pastry reads from screenshots, and the titles it gives them
  • Your settings, app rules and keyboard shortcuts

Screenshot reading and naming use Apple's on-device Vision and Apple Intelligence. Nothing is sent to Apple or anyone else to do it.

iCloud Sync

iCloud sync is optional and off until you turn it on, on each Mac and on your iPhone. When it is on, your history and collections are kept in your own private iCloud database, through Apple's CloudKit. We cannot see that database.

Everything you copied is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your device: text, links, images, names, titles, the text read from screenshots and which app something came from. Only your devices, signed in to your Apple Account, can read it. Apple can see only things like when an item was saved, what kind of item it is and how big it is.

Some things never go to iCloud at all: one-time codes, API keys, card numbers, screenshots that show one of those, and anything set to delete itself after a time.

Turning sync off stops it on that device. You can also remove everything Pastry stored in iCloud from Pastry's iCloud settings.

Pastry on iPhone

The iPhone app shows your history through iCloud sync. Things you add on the iPhone, with the paste button, the share sheet or the Shortcuts action, are kept in the app and sent to your own iCloud database when sync is on. Pastry never reads your iPhone's clipboard unless you tap paste.

How Your Privacy Is Protected

Secrets Expire

One-time codes, API keys and card numbers are deleted automatically after a time you choose, five minutes by default.

Password Managers Are Respected

Anything an app marks as private or temporary, as password managers do, is never saved.

Links and Photos Are Cleaned

Tracking codes are removed from links you copy, and location and camera details are removed from images, without changing the picture.

Hidden from Screen Sharing

Pastry keeps its window out of screenshots, screen sharing and most recordings.

Purchases

The one-time unlock and the yearly subscription are in-app purchases handled by Apple. Pastry learns only which one you have and, for the subscription, when it renews. It never sees your name, your email address or your payment details.

Accessibility Access

If you choose to let Pastry paste directly into other apps on your Mac, macOS asks you to grant Accessibility access. Pastry uses it only to press Command-V for you. It is optional, and Pastry works without it.

Data You Control

You can delete any item, delete your recent copies, or clear your history from Settings. With sync on, those deletions reach your other devices too. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device, and Pastry's iCloud settings can remove what it stored in iCloud.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, this page and the app's description in the App Store will be updated.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy, feel free to get in touch. The short version is simple: what you copy is yours, and only your devices can read it.
