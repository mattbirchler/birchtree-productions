Why good maps leave things out

Theo Park · Field Notes 9 min read

Every map is an argument about what matters. The cartographer who draws each hedge and footpath has made a map nobody can read. The skill is in choosing what to leave out, and trusting the reader to fill in the rest.

A map that shows everything is as useless as one that shows nothing at all. What you leave off the page is what lets the rest be seen.
