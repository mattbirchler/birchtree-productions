Fast

There before you finish the shortcut.

Press Command, Shift and V, type a few letters, press Return. Pastry appears at once, searches as you type, and pastes right where you were.

Private from the first launch

Out of your screenshots and meetings.

A clipboard manager sees everything you copy. Pastry is built around that fact, and every protection is on before you touch a setting.

Your history stays out of screenshots, screen sharing and most recordings. On the left, your screen. On the right, a screenshot taken at the same moment.

Privacy

What you copy stays yours.

Secrets delete themselves

One-time codes, API keys and card numbers are gone five minutes after you copy them.

Hidden in the list

Secrets show as dots until you ask to see one, so a glance over your shoulder gets nothing.

Clean links

Tracking codes are stripped from links the moment you copy them.

Clean photos

Location and camera details are removed from every image, without touching the picture.

Password managers respected

Anything a password manager marks as private is never saved at all.

No network at all

Pastry never connects to the internet. No account, no analytics, no servers.

And More

Small, with the things you need.

Collections

Keep links, snippets and addresses together, give items their own names, and open any collection with its own shortcut.

It knows what you copied

Colors show as swatches and paste as hex or RGB. Codes, cards, addresses and dates each get their own look.

Smaller images, same pixels

Screenshots shrink in the background at the same size and resolution, and Pastry tells you what it saved.

Searchable screenshots

The text inside every screenshot is read on your Mac, so search finds it.

Rules for each app

Never save from one app, always paste plain text into another. Set it once.

Keyboard first

Open, search, pick and paste without leaving the keys.
