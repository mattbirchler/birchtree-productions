Pick your platforms. Weave watches the limit.

The platform picker shows your actual @username for each connected account and uses each platform's real brand colors. Weave warns you if you've exceeded the most restrictive character limit across your selected platforms.

Every platform in one tap.

No switching between apps, no copy-pasting.

Threads that just work.

Triple-newline to split a post into a new thread item. No buttons, no drag-and-drop, just write naturally. A visual thread connector makes the structure obvious at a glance.

Each post gets its own character counter that's aware of which platform has the tightest limit, and its progress ring shifts from gray to accent to orange to red as you approach the limit.

Polish you can feel.

Type a time

Schedule in plain English.

"Tomorrow at 3pm", "in 2 hours", "next tuesday at 9am": type it in plain English and it parses on-device using Apple Foundation Models. No network call, no privacy concern.

February 2026

MONTUEWEDTHUFRISATSUN 1 2345678 9101112131415
Schedule for 2/16/26, 8:48 AM

Or pick a date, or join the queue.

There's also a graphical date picker as a fallback, plus "Add to Queue" to let Typefully auto-schedule your post in the next available slot.

A truly native iOS app.

Built with SwiftUI. Spring animations, haptic feedback, dynamic sheets, and system colors that adapt to light and dark mode. Switch the appearance, then tap Publish.
