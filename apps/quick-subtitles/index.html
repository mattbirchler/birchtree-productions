An hour-long podcast in less than a minute.

With Apple Intelligence built into the core experience, Quick Subtitles renders subtitles for an hour-long podcast in less than a minute on your iPhone. That's real-time transcription at 80x speed.

Your audio never touches the cloud.

Everything happens on-device. Your audio never gets uploaded to a server and is never shared with anyone: no servers, no cloud, no data collection. After the initial model download, you can transcribe anywhere with no internet at all.
